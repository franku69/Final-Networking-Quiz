# PRE-CLASS 10-MINUTE CHECK

Do this before students begin the graded section.

1. Confirm `https://franku69.github.io/Networking-Quiz/` opens on your PC.
2. Confirm `/teacher/` opens and connects to the live backend.
3. Create a **TEST** room.
4. Copy the room link and open it on one phone using mobile data.
5. Join, start, answer 2–3 questions, and confirm the CRM updates.
6. Submit the test and confirm the final score stays in CRM after the phone tab closes.
7. Delete or ignore the TEST room.
8. Create the real section room.
9. Keep the teacher CRM open and the backend online for the whole quiz.
10. Export the score CSV immediately after the section finishes.

If the backend says it is waking up, wait until the CRM shows LIVE CONNECTED before giving students the link.
